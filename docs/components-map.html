The loops

LOOP 1 · Research moreGray-band company: up to 3 extra same-site pages, each with a logged reason, then re-scored.
LOOP 2 · Fix the draftValidator fails: one regeneration with the failures, else rejected_draft. Every human edit is re-validated.
LOOP 3 · Follow-upsAfter N days, in the same thread, until the prospect replies, unsubscribes or bounces.
LOOP 4 · SuppressionUnsubscribes, bounces and complaints join the suppression list, checked before every send.
LOOP 5 · Learn from good repliesMark a sent email as a Good reply: it becomes a winner, and new drafts for similar people reuse its angle, structure and call to action. Facts always come from the new company's pages.
PLANNED · Find moreEvery run: if too few companies pass, take the next ranked ones from the feed pool, or web search if it is empty.
PLANNED · Learn from every editExtends Loop 5: every Gmail edit is captured as a before/after pair (pgvector) and retrieved like a good reply.
PLANNED · Learn monthlyHubSpot deals and revenue re-weight the ICP rubric and the feed keywords.
PLANNED · Kill switchPoor buyer results, or edit disagreement above 50% for 2 weeks → pause drafts and revert the prompt.
